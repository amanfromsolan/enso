## New
- Pinned tabs can be put to sleep. Waking one brings your Claude or Codex conversation right back.
- In splits, the pane you're typing in gets a blue ring and the others dim, so you always know where your keys go.
- The gap between panes is easier to grab, shows a hint when you hover it, and panes can't be squeezed down to nothing anymore.

## Fixed
- Reordering split tabs in the sidebar no longer breaks the split apart.
- With narrow split panes, you can drag and double-click the top of the window again.
- Resizing panes feels smoother.
