## New
- Splits are here: ⌘D puts a terminal side by side, ⇧⌘D stacks it, and dragging the gap between panes resizes them. These controls can also be found on top right. 
- Pinned tabs can be put to sleep, and waking one brings your Claude or Codex conversation right back. Yes, we love our users with low RAM.  
- Enso notifies you when an agent needs you, and can stay quiet while you're already in the app.
- Folders can go inside folders. Yes, we did not have it before. 
- Enso asks before closing a tab that still has something running, or an agent conversation it would discard. You can also tell Enso you like to YOLO and not to bother you again with these. 

## Improved
- Sidebar drag and drop was rebuilt: tabs drop anywhere, a clear line shows where they'll land, and folders light up when a tab would go inside.
- Enso brings back your five most recent agent tabs on launch and resumes the rest as you visit them, which you can change in settings.

## Fixed
- Selected text while renaming a tab was invisible in light mode.
