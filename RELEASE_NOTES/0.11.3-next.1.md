## New
- Find in the terminal. ⌘F opens a find bar on the focused pane and searches the whole scrollback, highlighting matches as you type. Suggested by [@mikerockett](https://github.com/mikerockett).
- Enter and ⇧Enter step through matches, Esc closes the bar and hands the keyboard back. Every pane in a split gets its own bar.
- ⌘E searches for the selected text, ⌘G and ⇧⌘G step between matches without leaving the terminal, and the search term follows the system find pasteboard, so a ⌘E in your editor and a ⌘F here just works.
