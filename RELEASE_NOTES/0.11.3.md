## New
- Find in the terminal. ⌘F searches the whole scrollback, ⌘E searches for what you've selected, and ⌘G jumps between matches. Suggested by [@mikerockett](https://github.com/mikerockett).
- A fresh sidebar: switch between all your Folders and just the tabs that are Active, with a glassy new bar for your spaces.
- Open folders sit in their own little tray, so you can see what belongs where.
- Flip between agents or terminals that need your attention from the top right.
- A Performance Bar for the curious. Turn on Developer tools in Settings, then open it from ⌘P.

## Improved
- Swiping between spaces is much smoother and arrives faster.
- Enso uses far less memory when you have lots of tabs open.
- Enso now runs on the latest Ghostty, bringing a pile of fixes and polish from the Ghostty team.
- App updates install more reliably on macOS 27.
