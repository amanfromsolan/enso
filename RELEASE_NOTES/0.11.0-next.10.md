## New
- Every pane header now carries its own controls at the far right: split right, split down, and close.
- The controls fade away while your mouse sits still and come back the moment you move it, so a pane you're reading stays uncluttered.
- Hover a control to see what it does and the shortcut that runs it.
- Narrow panes tuck the split controls into a ⋯ menu so close always has room.
