## New
- You can now drag to re-arrange spaces
- Double clicking a split border resets the split screen to its original ratio

Both suggested by Mike Rockett ([@mikerockett](https://github.com/mikerockett)). Thanks, Mike!

## Fixed
- Enso would expand all your folders on restart. Pretty silly. We told him not to do it.
- The sidebar now only opens when your pointer is on the left edge, not whenever it drifts nearby. Pointed out by [@eznix86](https://github.com/eznix86)
- In full screen the sidebar icon would linger. Now it hides with the sidebar as nature intended.
- Pressing Esc to close the command bar in full screen no longer kicks you out of full screen. Thanks to [@eznix86](https://github.com/eznix86) for the report.

Thank you for using Enso as your terminal!
